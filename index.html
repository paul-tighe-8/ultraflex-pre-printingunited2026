Questions

Do I have to attend PRINTING United to enter?
No. Anyone who prints wide format can enter. If you are coming to the show we will have samples ready for you at Booth C2461; if you are not, we will ship instead.
How does the credit work?
It is applied as a credit toward your next material order. Two awards: $5,000 on the banner side and $7,500 on the fabric side.
Why do you need my machine width and ink type?
Because credit is only worth having against media that runs on your equipment. Machine width and print technology are what decide which of our lines are actually relevant to you.
When does it close, and how are recipients chosen?
Entries close Friday 9 October at 5pm Pacific, two weeks after the hall closes, so anyone who did not reach the booth still has a route in. Entries are reviewed afterwards and recipients are announced Thursday 15 October.
Will somebody call me?
Only if you ask. Your details go to the Ultraflex rep who covers your territory so they know who you are, but entering is not a request for a sales call.
Can existing Ultraflex customers enter?
Yes. Tell us on the form so your rep knows you already buy from us and can pick up the conversation properly.
